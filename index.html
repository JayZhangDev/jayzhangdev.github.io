projects

FuturesBotSuite futures · research framework

A paper-trading bot for MES (Micro S&P 500) futures running an Opening Range Breakout strategy under real prop-firm risk rules, plus a full research framework I built around it: an automated data recorder, a backtesting engine with honest fills and cost modeling, a research logger, and a market-regime dashboard.

PythonpandasyfinanceSQLitebacktestingrisk modeling
What I learned: my first backtest looked great — then I found it was filling entries at prices I couldn't actually get and ignoring costs. I rebuilt the engine to fill honestly and model commission + slippage. The numbers got worse, and far more trustworthy. Also learned that 28 trades tells you nothing — sample size is everything.

MemeBotFinal solana · live trading

A Solana memecoin trading bot with automated rug-pull detection, position sizing, trailing stops, a backtester, and a GUI — built to operate in fast-moving on-chain markets while managing the constant risk of getting rugged.

PythonSolanaon-chain dataGUIrisk management
What I learned: real markets punish sloppy assumptions. Backtests that look profitable can hide overfitting and subtle data leakage — separating "this has edge" from "this got lucky" is the whole game, and it's harder than writing the strategy.
